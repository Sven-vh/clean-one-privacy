English

This policy covers the games Blobs, Breach, Chains, Crossings, Flood, 4 in a Row, Hexagon Minesweeper, Rings, Shape Crush, Square Blast, Strides, Tao and Twists, made by Sven van Huessen ("we"). They are built to need nothing from you.

What we collect: nothing. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Purchases. Colour themes are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only asks Google Play which colours you own, so it can unlock them (also after a reinstall).

Internet. The games work offline. They only go online to talk to Google Play for purchases, or when you open a store page from "More puzzles".

Children. The games are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone.

Changes. If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.

Contact. svanhuessen@gmail.com
